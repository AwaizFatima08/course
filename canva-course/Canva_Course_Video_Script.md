# Canva from Zero to a Digital Freelance Career: Video Script (English and Urdu)

**Format:** one video per chapter, 4 to 6 minutes each, 16:9, 1920 × 1080.
**Style:** screen-recorded Canva walkthroughs with animated course-drawing cards between steps.
**Narration:** English and Urdu on separate tracks, using the same visuals.
**Text on screen:** short captions in English, with Urdu captions in the Urdu version.

## Production rules (apply to every chapter)

- Hide personal details (email, name, profile photo) on every screen recording.
- Use a fresh demo account. Do not show real client work or real business details.
- Show the Canva logo and screens only as the course describes them. Check Canva's brand guidelines before publishing.
- Keep one idea per scene. Each scene should show one action and one result.
- Put the chapter number and title on the first 3 seconds of every video.
- Keep a 5-second end card: "Practise: [exercise name]. Next chapter: [title]."

**Scene key used below:** `[SCREEN]` = screen recording of Canva. `[CARD]` = animated course drawing. `[TITLE]` = text card. `[CAPTION]` = on-screen text.

## Chapter 5: Design Fundamentals You Can Apply in Canva

**Duration:** 6 minutes
**Visuals:**
1. `[CARD]` Figure 5.1, rule of thirds: the grid appears, then the headline snaps to a line.
2. `[SCREEN]` Before and after: a cluttered poster, then the same poster with one headline, three colours and two fonts.
3. `[CARD]` Figure 5.2, visual hierarchy: the text sizes step down.
4. `[CARD]` Figure 5.3, three colour schemes (complementary, analogous, triadic).
5. `[TITLE]` Contrast rule: body text needs at least 4.5 to 1 contrast.

**English narration:**
> Good design is about why things sit where they are. Use the rule of thirds to place your focal point. Four principles hold every layout together: alignment, proximity, repetition and contrast. Limit yourself to two fonts and one clear size for each level. Start your palette with one main colour, one support colour and a neutral. For body text, aim for at least 4.5 to 1 contrast, so people can read it easily.

**Urdu narration:**
> اچھا ڈیزائن یہ جاننے کا نام ہے کہ چیزیں وہاں کیوں رکھی گئی ہیں۔ تہائی کے اصول سے اپنا مرکزی نقطہ رکھیں۔ چار اصول ہر لے آؤٹ کو جوڑتے ہیں: سیدھ، قربت، تکرار اور تضاد۔ دو فونٹس تک محدود رہیں، اور ہر درجے کا ایک واضح سائز رکھیں۔ ایک بنیادی رنگ، ایک معاون رنگ اور ایک نیوٹرل رنگ چنیں۔ باڈی ٹیکسٹ کے لیے کم از کم 4.5 سے 1 کا تضاد رکھیں تاکہ پڑھنا آسان ہو۔

**Exercise on screen:** Rebuild a poorly designed flyer using the four principles.

## Chapter 8: Building a Portfolio and Personal Brand

**Duration:** 5 minutes
**Visuals:**
1. `[CARD]` Figure 8.1, portfolio structure, sections appearing one by one.
2. `[SCREEN]` A case study layout: Problem, Approach, Output, Result.
3. `[TITLE]` "Be honest: label practice work as practice."
4. `[SCREEN]` Export the portfolio as PDF.

**English narration:**
> Clients hire on proof, not on software lists. Choose six to eight strong pieces. For each one, write a short case study: the problem, your approach, the output, and the result. ## Chapter 9: Pricing, Finding Clients and Communicating

**Duration:** 6 minutes
**Visuals:**
1. `[CARD]` Pricing models table, one row at a time.
2. `[SCREEN]` Worked example on a whiteboard-style card: 1,100 ÷ 50 = 22 per billable hour.
3. `[CARD]` Package tiers: Basic, Standard (most popular), Premium.
4. `[SCREEN]` Outreach message on a phone screen (demo name, no real business).
5. `[CAPTION]` "Deposit: 30 to 50 percent before starting new client work."

**English narration:**
> Start your price from the income you need. Add your business costs, then divide by the hours you can actually bill. Not every working hour is paid, because selling and admin take time too. Here, 1,100 divided by 50 billable hours gives 22 per hour. That is a starting point. Then price by value. Offer three packages, and make the middle one the best value. For a new client, ask for a deposit before you start.

**Urdu narration:**
> قیمت اس آمدنی سے شروع کریں جس کی آپ کو ضرورت ہے۔ کاروباری اخراجات شامل کریں، پھر ان قابلِ ادائیگی گھنٹوں سے تقسیم کریں جو آپ واقعی بل کر سکتے ہیں۔ ہر کام کا وقت معاوضے والا نہیں ہوتا۔ یہاں 1,100 کو 50 قابلِ ادائیگی گھنٹوں سے تقسیم کریں تو فی گھنٹہ 22 بنتا ہے۔ یہ ابتدائی نقطہ ہے۔ پھر قدر کی بنیاد پر قیمت رکھیں۔ تین پیکیج پیش کریں۔ نئے کلائنٹ سے شروع کرنے سے پہلے پیشگی رقم لیں۔

**Exercise on screen:** Calculate your own minimum hourly rate and write a three-tier package.

## Chapter 12: Similar Platforms and Transferable Skills

**Visuals:**

2. `[CARD]` Transferable skills: brief reading, hierarchy, colour systems, file management, communication.
3. `[SCREEN]` The same poster rebuilt in Google Slides or Adobe Express, side by side with Canva.
4. `[CAPTION]` "Check each vendor's site for current prices and availability."

**English narration:**
> Canva is one tool in a larger ecosystem. The skills you learned, such as layout, hierarchy, colour and brand consistency, work in every design tool. Learning a second tool is easier than the first. Adobe Express and Google Slides are simple next steps. Figma is popular for app and website design. Illustrator suits logos and icons. CapCut helps with short videos.
