A COMPLETE BEGINNER-TO-PROFESSIONAL COURSE

Building a Digital Career Online

How to use freelancing and remote-work platforms such as Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer, Toptal and more — create accounts, hunt for jobs, attract clients and grow a career from home.

Who this course is for: anyone with basic computer and internet skills who wants to earn online.
Language: English    Edition: November 2026    Chapters: 18 in 4 parts

Platform fees, features and rules change often. Figures in this course were checked against public sources in November 2026 but are marked as approximate. Always confirm on the platform's own help or pricing page before you act on them.
